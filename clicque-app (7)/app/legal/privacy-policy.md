## What we collect
- **Nickname and avatar** you choose. A photo avatar stays on your device and is not uploaded.
- **Gameplay and chat data** in online rooms: your messages, votes and room activity. Rooms are deleted automatically after about 24 hours.
- **Anonymous account ID** created automatically so the game can tell players apart. We do not collect your name, email or phone number.
- **Reports** you send about other players (the message, the player ID, the room).
- **Basic technical data** (device type, crash logs, IP address) processed by our hosting providers.

## How we use it
To run online games, show chat to players in the same room, keep the service safe (filtering, reports, abuse prevention), and fix bugs.

## AI-written characters
In some games, AI characters write chat messages. To do this, the game state and recent chat of your room are sent to Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, through our server. We do not send your name or contact details. You can turn this off in Settings.

## On-device learning
If you leave "Characters learn from my play" on, the game keeps a small summary of your style on your device (for example common words and tactics). Part of it is included in the AI chat requests described above. You can switch it off or delete it in Settings.

## Returning cast
By default, the AI contestants and what they remember about you and each other (relationships, voting history) are saved on your device so the next game continues the story. You can switch this off or delete it in Settings.
